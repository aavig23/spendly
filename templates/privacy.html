Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect your name, email address, and password. When you use the service, we store the expense entries you add, including the amount, category, date, and any description you provide. We only collect the information needed to run the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate your account and to show you your expenses, category breakdowns, and spending summaries. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in Spendly's database. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse, although no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You may delete your expenses or close your account at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers. Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and your IP address may be visible to Google when those fonts are loaded.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of Spendly after changes are made constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team.
